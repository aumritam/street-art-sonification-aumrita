Instructions
  1. Test the colors: Move your mouse over the palette.
  2. Start eye tracking: Click the blue button and allow camera access.
  3. Calibrate: Look directly at each red dot and click it five times. Keep your head relatively still.
  4. Test your gaze: Once calibration finishes, look around the canvas.
  5. Fix a wrong spot: If the ring is off, look at a spot and click it. Every click teaches the tracker. The Friction slider makes the ring steadier (higher) or quicker (lower).
  6. Switch or stop: Use Back to mouse tracking to compare against your cursor (the same button switches back to your eyes), or Stop eye tracking to pause. Resuming keeps your calibration.
  7. Music mode: Click Music mode to turn colors into chords. Where you look (or hover with the mouse) decides the chord: hue picks the note, saturation picks how many notes, and red / green / blue picks major / minor / sus. It works with eye tracking or mouse mode.

Webcam access requires HTTPS or a supported localhost address. Eye-tracking accuracy depends on lighting, camera position, and calibration.
